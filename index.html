About the book

Warm, whimsical, and full of wonder

A Wiener Afloat is a charming illustrated story inspired by Scooby, a real dachshund who lived aboard a sailboat and wandered the beautiful Sea of Cortez.

Written by Samantha Terry, this story celebrates the adventurous spirit of dogs, the beauty of nature, and the joy of childhood imagination.

“A little pup, a bigger adventure, and a whole lot of love for the sea.”
