About the story

Inspired by a real-life sailing dog

A Wiener Afloat began with a simple idea: tell the story of a brave little dachshund who lived aboard a sailboat and saw the world from a new perspective.

Scooby, the real dog behind the story, embodies curiosity, charm, and courage. He isn’t just a pet in this tale — he is a companion, a sailor, and a friend who made adventure feel like home.

Samantha Terry wrote this story to bring the beauty of the Sea of Cortez to life for young readers, blending emotional warmth with vivid imagery and a playful sense of wonder.
